## Contract at this boundary

| Aspect | Local contract |
| --- | --- |
| Responsibilities | Preserve the stated local role and make the relevant Vox logical-contract binding inspectable. |
| Inputs | Acoustic-domain query, evidence source references, terminology/context, policy-redaction constraints, and response scope. |
| Outputs | Evidence-grounded interpretation, source/limitation references, and a provenance-aware response. |
| Policy / trust | Consume relevant identity, trust, transport, placement, and data-policy decisions without redefining their authority. |
| Provenance / receipt | Preserve consequential input, configuration, outcome, limitation, and receipt references; record any redaction or retention restriction. |

| Non-ownership | This repository does not own Vox canonical semantics, global identity/trust authority, or other shared systems consumed at this boundary. |
